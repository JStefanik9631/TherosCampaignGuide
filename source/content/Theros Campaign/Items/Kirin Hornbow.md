**Current Owner:** [[Henrik]]

**Type**: Bow (requires attunement)

**Effect:** This bow is made from elements of a radiant horn, and is strung with a fine braid of unicorn hair. You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. 

The weapon has 10 charges and regains all expended charges daily at dawn. Whenever you make an attack with the bow, you can expend 1 of its charges as part of the attack to make the roll with advantage. If the attack hits, it deals an extra 1d10 radiant damage, and any friendly creatures within 5 feet of the target gain a number of temporary hit points equal to the number rolled on the d10. If you roll a 10 on the die, the radius of the effect increases to 30 feet.
