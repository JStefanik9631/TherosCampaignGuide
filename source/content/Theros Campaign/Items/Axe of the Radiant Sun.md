**Current Owner:** [[Corvel]]

**Type**: Battleaxe (requires attunement)

**Effect:** This golden axe is always warm to the touch, as if it had been resting in the sun. You gain a +2 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon between dawn and dusk, or a +1 bonus between dusk and dawn. 

**Radiant Swing.** When you take the Attack action while wielding the axe, you can forgo one of your attacks to instead send a burning wave of brilliant light from the weapon's edge in a 15-foot cone. Each creature within that area must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 3d6 radiant damage, plus an extra 1d6 fire damage if it's between dawn and dusk. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage. The fire ignites flammable objects in the area that aren't being worn or carried. Once this property of the axe has been used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.
