| Beneath the sky where tempests roar,  <br>Where stars in endless patterns soar,  <br>Phenax spoke with a cunning grin,  <br>"A game of fate—shall we begin?                   |                                                                                            |
| Grant mortal minds a touch of flame,  <br>And see if ruin takes their name.  <br>Inspire their hearts, unseen, unknown,  <br>And watch the seeds of chaos sown."              | Phenax giving mortals a gift/curse and seeing what it would do to them.                    |
| The silent god, their face concealed,  <br>Agreed to see what fate revealed.  <br>With unseen hand, they cast the spark,  <br>And lit the path both bright and dark.          | Silent God may be [[Athreos]]. Spark? Path to the [[Underworld]]?                          |
| Eight souls arose, their hearts alight,  <br>Each cursed to weave the threads of blight.                                                                                      |                                                                                            |
| The first awoke with painted guise,  <br>Their words a web of truth and lies.  <br>Their stage became a treacherous snare,  <br>And none could see the danger there.          |                                                                                            |
| The second brewed in shadowed glow,  <br>A poison none would dare to know.  <br>With whispered words and secrets vile,  <br>They labored long to beguile.                     |                                                                                            |
| Another shaped with hands of gold,  <br>Devices vast, both new and old.  <br>Their wonders spun from boundless dreams,  <br>Became machines of fatal schemes.                 | Shaped by hands of gold, by the King?<br>Edrin has boundless dreams? (Should we kill him?) |
| A sailor braved the tempest’s tide,  <br>Where unseen horrors seethe and bide.  <br>They mapped a course through waters cursed,  <br>And doomed the world with each traverse. |                                                                                            |
| Deep in the earth, with lantern’s gleam,  <br>Another sought a fractured dream.  <br>Through depths unknown, their hands laid bare  <br>The ancient power slumbering there.   |                                                                                            |
| Their shadow looms where kinfolk tread,  <br>A whispered name the wise still dread.  <br>Their will commands, their gaze consumes,  <br>A living curse the bloodline blooms.  | Thornwells and their bloodline.                                                            |
| A thief of breath, unseen, untold,  <br>Their touch as sharp as winter’s cold.  <br>Through shadowed streets, they wove their way,  <br>And stole the dawn from breaking day. |                                                                                            |
| But one was shrouded, name and face,  <br>A fleeting ghost, a hollow space.  <br>Even gods could not define  <br>The source of this enigma’s line.                            |                                                                                            |
| And so the world, in silence, turned,  <br>As fires rose and futures burned.  <br>What gods began, the mortals sealed,  <br>A truth untamed, a fate revealed.                 |                                                                                            |
